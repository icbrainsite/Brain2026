Conference Venue

BRAIN'26 will be hosted in hybrid mode at the Netaji Subhas University of Technology (NSUT) campus, Dwarka, New Delhi

About NSUT Campus

Netaji Subhas University of Technology (NSUT) is one of India's premier technical universities, located in Dwarka, New Delhi. The Department of Management Studies (DMS) and the Department of Innovation, Entrepreneurship & Venture Development (DIEV) will jointly host BRAIN'26 on the NSUT main campus, offering delegates a modern, well-connected venue in the heart of the capital.

The conference will be conducted in hybrid mode — delegates may attend in person at NSUT or participate online, ensuring accessibility for our national and international collaborators, including Central University of Technology, Free State, South Africa.

How to Reach NSUT

Metro, airport and railway connectivity

By Metro

Nearest metro station: Dwarka Mor on the Blue Line, well connected to the rest of Delhi NCR. Auto-rickshaws and cabs are readily available from the station to the NSUT campus.

Nearest Metro: Dwarka Mor

By Air

The venue is well connected to Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL). Prepaid taxis, app-based cabs and the Airport Metro Express are all convenient options for reaching Dwarka.

Airport Distance: To be confirmed

By Rail

Delegates arriving by train can reach NSUT from New Delhi Railway Station via metro or taxi, with onward connectivity to Dwarka Mor on the Blue Line.

Railway Distance: To be confirmed

Pleasant Winter Conditions

New Delhi experiences cool, pleasant winter weather in late December, with daytime temperatures typically ranging from 10°C to 20°C. Mornings and evenings can be chilly and foggy — delegates are advised to carry warm clothing.
