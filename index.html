About BRAIN'26

Driving Inclusive Sustainability Through Innovation, Culture & Technology

Inclusive Sustainability

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What is the conference registration fee?

Registration fees vary by category: Students/Research Scholars ₹2,500, Academicians ₹3,000, Industry ₹4,000, Non-Author ₹1,200, and Foreign Delegates $50. Registration is open from 1 September to 26 November 2026.

How do I submit a research paper?

Visit our Submission page to upload your Full Paper or Extended Abstract in APA 7 format. Submissions open 18 August 2026 and close 25 October 2026. All papers undergo double-blind peer review.

Will accommodations be provided?

The conference will provide information on partner hotels in New Delhi. Participants can book accommodations directly. We recommend booking early as rooms fill up quickly.

Is there virtual participation available?

Yes, we offer virtual participation options for international attendees. Virtual registration includes access to live-streamed sessions and digital materials.

What publication opportunities are available?

Selected papers presented at BRAIN'26 will be considered for publication in reputed indexed journals, subject to rigorous double-blind peer review. Full details are available on the Submission page.

Can I get a refund if I cancel my registration?

Refunds are available up to 30 days before the conference. Cancellations after December 21, 2026 are not eligible for refunds. A processing fee may apply.
