About NSUT

Netaji Subhas University of Technology (NSUT) is one of India's leading technical universities, located in Dwarka, New Delhi. Renowned for its academic rigor and research output, NSUT hosts a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs across engineering, technology and management disciplines. The university provides the vibrant, well-connected campus that will host BRAIN'26 in hybrid mode.

DMS
Image Placeholder

About DMS

The Department of Management Studies (DMS) at NSUT is dedicated to nurturing future business leaders through rigorous academic programs and impactful research in strategic management, finance, marketing, human resources and related fields. DMS serves as a lead organizer of BRAIN'26, curating the conference's academic tracks and review process.

DIEV
Image Placeholder
Co-Organizer

About DIEV

The Department of Innovation, Entrepreneurship & Venture Development (DIEV) at NSUT champions the entrepreneurial ecosystem — supporting startups, industry collaboration and venture creation. As co-organizer of BRAIN'26, DIEV brings a strong focus on innovation, entrepreneurship and industry linkages to the conference's tracks and keynote sessions.

CUT South Africa
Image Placeholder
Collaborating Partner

About CUT, South Africa

Central University of Technology (CUT), based in Free State, South Africa, is a leading institution known for its focus on technology-driven education and applied research. As BRAIN'26's international collaborating partner, CUT contributes South African perspectives on inclusive sustainability, innovation and entrepreneurship, strengthening the conference's global relevance.
