Terms & Cancellation Policy

Please review before completing your registration

Terms & Conditions

  • Registration is confirmed only after successful payment and form submission.
  • At least one author must register to present an accepted paper.
  • Registration category must match the delegate's actual status (student, academician, industry, or foreign delegate).
  • Certificates will be issued only to registered and attending delegates.

Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to icbrain@nsut.ac.in.
  • Refund eligibility and processing timelines will be communicated by the organizing committee on a case-by-case basis.
  • No cancellations will be accepted after 26 November 2026 (registration close).
  • Registration fees are non-transferable to other individuals unless approved by the committee.

Registration FAQs

Common questions about registering for BRAIN'26

When does registration open and close?

Registration opens on 1 September 2026 and closes on 26 November 2026.

Do I need to register before my paper is accepted?

No — registration can be completed after acceptance notification (1 November 2026), but must be done before 26 November 2026 to secure your presentation slot.

Can I attend without presenting a paper?

Yes — select the "Non-Author (Attendee Only)" category at ₹1,200 to attend without presenting.

How do foreign delegates pay the registration fee?

Foreign delegates can pay the $50 fee via international bank transfer using the Swift code provided, or through the payment link shared on the registration form.

Will I receive a confirmation after registering?

Yes — a confirmation email will be sent to your registered email address once your payment and form submission are verified.

Who do I contact for registration issues?

Email icbrain@nsut.ac.in or visit our Contact page for assistance.
